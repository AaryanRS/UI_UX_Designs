QUESTIONS & PROTOCOLE

How are the botanical extractions harvested? +
Our extractions are gathered by hand strictly at the diurnal threshold—either within 40 minutes before sunrise or immediately following dusk. At these specific hours, volatile terpene losses are minimized, preserving raw botanical complexity that machine harvesting inherently fractures.
Why do flacons vary slightly in colour and viscosity? +
Because PARRIS employs unrectified botanical absolutes, soil minerals, rainfall abundance, and solar intensity impart natural variations to every individual harvest. We refuse industrial bleaching, chemical clarifiers, or artificial coloring agents; the liquid reflects the honest condition of that season's earth.
What is the recommended maturation on the skin? +
Unlike commercial atomizers intended for instant aerosol projection, our formulas develop intimately over hours. Apply directly to pulse points and avoid rubbing. Allow fifteen minutes for the grain carrier to dissipate before evaluating the initial top accords.
International private courier and climate-controlled dispatch. +
All reserve orders ship securely in temperature-stabilized wooden cases sealed with natural beeswax and numbered ledger documentation. Dispatches are fulfilled weekly directly from our formulation vaults in Grasse and Paris VII.
